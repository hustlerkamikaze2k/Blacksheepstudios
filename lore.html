Where worlds collide

The story behind the show.

Two worlds. Ricky Spanish lives in the cartoon world — loud, bright, and larger than life. But the cartoon world isn't enough for him. Ricky would give anything to be in the real world, all the time.
The girl who wants out. In the real world there's a girl who's tired of it — depressed, worn down, done. She wants out of the real world. Ricky wants in. They love each other, and they were made for each other — but the one thing each of them wants most is the opposite of what the other wants.
The blimp. The neon "Ricky Spanish" blimp crosses from world to world — and most of the time, it's going to pick up Chloe. That's how the two worlds touch: a little at a time, a taste of each other's lives.
The show. When Ricky comes to the real world, he hosts. The audience stampedes into the BlackSheep Studios mansion, up the grand staircase, into the studio — and Ricky runs the room. Real people. Real opinions. No filter.
